## About Me

I'm an Analytics Engineer / Data Engineer with over 18 years in Marketing & Sales, including roughly a decade in Market Insights and Analytics roles — building marketing databases, automating reporting, and running market/competitive intelligence on large market data models. As that work grew increasingly data-driven, I completed further training as an Analytics Engineer at Masterschool, and for the past two years I've been working full-time in that role.

## What I Do

Today I work as a full-stack Analytics Engineer for a Frankfurt-based company, owning data products end-to-end:

* Building Python-based extraction scripts that pull data from source systems into Snowflake
* Modeling the full transformation layer in dbt across the Bronze, Silver, and Gold medallion layers
* Delivering secure, governed data into downstream data marts
* Building reports for different stakeholder groups in Power BI

I'm the direct point of contact for stakeholders across the organization — from developers and database maintainers on the team level, up to B-level and C-level leadership — and I trained the team in dbt fundamentals through a self-built 8-week internal training program.

**Tools & Technologies:** Snowflake, dbt, Power BI, AWS, Python
